The read

The survey is not the problem. Nearly nine in ten who start it finish all twenty questions. Whatever you change, don’t change that.

The landing page is the first leak. Two thirds of arrivals never press start. Ten of twenty-eight sessions landed and left without a single further event.

Saving is the real leak. Twelve sessions reached the course list and three saved anything. That’s the moment the product is supposed to earn its keep, and it mostly doesn’t — either the star isn’t visible enough, or a student who has just met a real course doesn’t yet see why keeping it matters.

People came back. Seven sessions browsed courses without taking the survey at all — their result was already in storage. That is the most encouraging signal here and it isn’t visible in any single rate.

You have no qualitative data. Zero feedback across every session, so these twenty-eight lines are everything you know, and they can’t tell you why anyone left. At this sample size, five screen-shares will teach you more than another month of counting.
